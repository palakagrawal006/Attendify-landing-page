How it works

One platform, two journeys

STEP 01

Create a class and share it

Create a subject with its code and section, then share a link or QR code. Students join on their own, with no manual lists.

STEP 02

Take attendance in seconds

Pick a subject, then capture or upload classroom photos, or run a hands-free voice roll call. Matching runs only against that subject's enrolled students.

STEP 03

Review and save

Review every match with the photo it came from before you confirm. Absent students are recorded too, all under one shared timestamp.

STEP 04

Reports that explain themselves

Every session is logged with its time, subject and a present count, so past attendance is always one click away.

STEP 01

Register with your face

One camera capture creates your account, and an optional voice sample adds a second factor. After that, logging in takes one glance.

STEP 02

Join a class

Open the teacher's link, scan the QR code, or type the class code. You're enrolled in one tap.

STEP 03

Get marked present

Your face or voice is verified during the teacher's session, so there is no sign-in sheet to pass around.

STEP 04

Track your own record

See every subject you're enrolled in with your attendance, and unenroll any time.
